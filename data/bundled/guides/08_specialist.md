# Other specialist areas

VIDHAAN source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Information Technology, Environment Protection and Right to Information Act collections.

Facts needed for case-specific research: Specific issue and sector, location, dates, records, authority and applicable proceedings.

Coverage gaps: Specialist coverage is a sample: complete data-protection, intellectual-property, sector-regulatory and environmental notification/case-law collections are absent.

## Example local search

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m vidhaan.cli search 'Find legal recognition of electronic records in the Information Technology Act.' --domain 'Other specialist areas'
```

Windows uses `.venv\Scripts\python.exe`. Effective-date filters exclude unknown versions. Reviewed research currently returns a gap.
