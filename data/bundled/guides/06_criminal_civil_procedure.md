# Criminal and civil procedure

Original source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: BNS, BNSS, BSA, historical Evidence Act, limitation, General Clauses, prevention of corruption, advocates, securities Special Court, debt recovery and economic-offence limitation.

Facts needed: Nature of matter, court, location, offence/cause-of-action dates, procedural stage and relevant orders.

Coverage gaps: The complete Code of Civil Procedure, court rules and current transition/commencement notifications are not supplied. The three criminal Acts are Gazette snapshots, not certified current consolidations.
