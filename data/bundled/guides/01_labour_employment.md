# Labour and employment

VIDHAAN source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Plantation welfare, bonded labour, contract-labour administration and department explanations of Labour Codes.

Facts needed for case-specific research: Establishment type, employment category, location, relevant dates and contract facts.

Coverage gaps: The full four Labour Codes and state employment laws are not supplied. Historical Acts and FAQ explanations do not establish current applicability.
