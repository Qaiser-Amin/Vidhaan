# Corporate and tax

VIDHAAN source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Companies and limited-liability-partnership Act collections, plus official Income-tax Act transition FAQs.

Facts needed for case-specific research: Entity type, incorporation details, transaction date, tax/assessment year, residence, relevant returns and notices.

Coverage gaps: The FAQ is department guidance, not the full Income-tax Act. GST, complete current tax rates, corporate rules and all amendments are not supplied.

## Example local search

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m vidhaan.cli search 'Find the Companies Act passage on incorporation of a company.' --domain 'Corporate and tax'
```

Windows uses `.venv\Scripts\python.exe`. Effective-date filters exclude unknown versions. Reviewed research currently returns a gap.
