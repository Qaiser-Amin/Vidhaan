# Family law

Original source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Hindu marriage, succession, adoption/maintenance and minority/guardianship, plus Special Marriage; selected related family-property, trust, partition, BNSS maintenance and historical marital-evidence passages.

Facts needed: Applicable personal-law framework, relationships, domicile, dates, family/property facts and relevant proceedings.

Coverage gaps: Muslim, Christian and other personal-law frameworks, Family Courts Act and complete family-court procedure are absent. Related marital evidence includes the historical Evidence Act, whose transition/applicability is unreviewed.

5 primary documents and 5 related documents are included. Related scope is an editorial passage selection, not legal review. Shared documents are stored once.
