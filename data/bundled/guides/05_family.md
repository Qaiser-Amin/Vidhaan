# Family law

VIDHAAN source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Hindu marriage and Hindu succession collections, including marriage conditions, divorce and inheritance passages.

Facts needed for case-specific research: Applicable personal-law framework, relationships, domicile, dates, family/property facts and relevant proceedings.

Coverage gaps: Special Marriage, Muslim, Christian and other personal laws, adoption/guardianship laws and family-court procedure are not comprehensively supplied.

| Collected document | Format and extent | Review |

## Example local search

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m vidhaan.cli search 'Find the Hindu Succession Act passage on devolution of interest in coparcenary property.' --domain 'Family law'
```

Windows uses `.venv\Scripts\python.exe`. Effective-date filters exclude unknown versions. Reviewed research currently returns a gap.
