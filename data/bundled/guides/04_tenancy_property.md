# Tenancy and property

Original source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Property transfers and leases, RERA, easements, trusts, partition, Benami property, registration, land acquisition and charitable trusts/endowments.

Facts needed: State or union territory, property use, tenancy type, agreement, rent, transaction dates and any notices.

Coverage gaps: State rent-control, tenancy, land-revenue and local real-estate rules are not collected. Central lease passages alone cannot decide a state eviction question.
