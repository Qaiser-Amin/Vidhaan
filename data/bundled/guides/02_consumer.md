# Consumer law

VIDHAAN source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Consumer definitions, complaints, consumer commissions, remedies, mediation and product liability in the department-hosted 2019 Act collection.

Facts needed for case-specific research: Transaction and complaint dates, goods or services, parties, location, value and alleged defect or deficiency.

Coverage gaps: Rules, current pecuniary-jurisdiction notifications, later amendments and consumer decisions are not comprehensively collected.
