# Contracts

VIDHAAN source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Agreements, consent, breach, specific performance, injunctions and sale-of-goods conditions in three Act collections.

Facts needed for case-specific research: Complete agreement, parties and capacity, governing law, performance dates, alleged breach and remedy sought.

Coverage gaps: These collections do not supply a complete case-law interpretation, sector-specific contracts or later applicable amendments.

| Collected document | Format and extent | Review |
|---|---|---|
| [Indian Contract Act, 1872](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/indian-contract-act-1872) | English section collection; 193 records | unreviewed |
| [Sale of Goods Act, 1930](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/sale-of-goods-act-1930) | English section collection; 67 records | unreviewed |
| [Specific Relief Act, 1963](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/specific-relief-act-1963) | English section collection; 49 records | unreviewed |

## Example local search

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m vidhaan.cli search 'Find compensation for loss or damage caused by breach of contract.' --domain 'Contracts'
```

Windows uses `.venv\Scripts\python.exe`. Effective-date filters exclude unknown versions. Reviewed research currently returns a gap.
