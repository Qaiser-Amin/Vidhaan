# Corpus reproduction and attribution

VIDHAAN includes selected statutory texts together with original research guides by Qaiser Amin Ganaie. Every bundled source has an accompanying guide, source URL, original checksum, attribution and a scoped reproduction basis in `data/bundled/manifest.json`. Keep the guides and this notice with the source collection.

The [Copyright Act, 1957, section 52](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-52-114), published in the Income Tax Department's Act collection, provides for reproduction of Acts together with commentary or other original matter in section 52(1)(q)(ii). The project's eight original domain guides supply that accompanying matter. Gazette material is addressed separately by section 52(1)(q)(i); the Contract Labour Rules document reproduces G.S.R. 190/191, and each consumer Rules/Regulations/Guidelines document retains its own inspected Gazette reference in the manifest. The Special Marriage Act PDF is hosted by India Code; its statutory text also accompanies the original family guide.

The official tax-transition FAQ is included under the Income Tax Department's [copyright policy](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/copyright-policy), which permits accurate reproduction with prominent acknowledgment and excludes identified third-party copyright. Its source is the Directorate of Income Tax / CBDT, Ministry of Finance, Government of India.

Some department-hosted Act text carries Taxmann notices. Those notices remain intact. The statutory-text reproduction basis does not grant a blanket licence to Taxmann books, publisher commentary, database software, site designs or unrelated material. The bundle contains the inspected legal documents and verified plain-text section records; original HTML/API payloads stay in the acquisition workspace. Acquisition response hashes and section links remain in the compact receipts. The rendering's original acquisition header should be read with this distinction.

Two Labour Ministry FAQs (`labour-faq-2025-12`, `labour-faq-2026-03`) are omitted from the distributed corpus: the Ministry [copyright policy](https://www.labour.gov.in/policies/copyright-policy) requires permission for reproduction. The acquisition manifest retains their official URLs for appropriate local use. No permission is invented or transferred by VIDHAAN.

The Ministry of Home Affairs [website policy](https://www.mha.gov.in/en/page/website-policy) also restricts general website reproduction. Its three bundled Gazette Acts are included as legislative texts accompanied by the original guides, rather than under a claimed general website licence.

These records concern reproduction scope, not legal review of the laws. All source versions remain unreviewed; effective dates and current applicability remain unknown. Models, dependencies and user-uploaded private documents retain their own rights and are not part of this corpus bundle.

Only the eight manifest-declared official BM25 inverted indexes are redistributed. They are verified against bundled source/chunk fingerprints. Runtime indexes, user uploads and model weights remain excluded.
