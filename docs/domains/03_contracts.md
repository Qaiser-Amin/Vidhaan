# Contracts

Original source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Agreements, remedies, sale of goods, arbitration, partnerships, negotiable instruments, stamp duties, MSME, securities contracts and security enforcement.

Facts needed: Complete agreement, parties and capacity, governing law, performance dates, alleged breach and remedy sought.

Coverage gaps: These collections do not supply a complete case-law interpretation, sector-specific contracts or later applicable amendments.
