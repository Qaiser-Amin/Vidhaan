# Criminal and civil procedure

VIDHAAN source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: English Gazette texts of BNS, BNSS and BSA, plus the Limitation Act collection for suits, appeals and applications.

Facts needed for case-specific research: Nature of matter, court, location, offence/cause-of-action dates, procedural stage and relevant orders.

Coverage gaps: The complete Code of Civil Procedure, court rules and current transition/commencement notifications are not supplied. The three criminal Acts are Gazette snapshots, not certified current consolidations.

## Example local search

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m vidhaan.cli search 'Find the Limitation Act passage about extension of the prescribed period in certain cases.' --domain 'Criminal and civil procedure'
```

Windows uses `.venv\Scripts\python.exe`. Effective-date filters exclude unknown versions. Reviewed research currently returns a gap.
