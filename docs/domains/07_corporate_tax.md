# Corporate and tax

Original source-linked research guide. Author: Qaiser Amin Ganaie.

Collected scope: Companies, LLPs, Income Tax Act 2025, FEMA, SEBI, depositories, competition, accountancy/company-secretary professions, Black Money, multi-state cooperatives and official tax-transition FAQ.

Facts needed: Entity type, incorporation details, transaction date, tax/assessment year, residence, relevant returns and notices.

Coverage gaps: The FAQ is department guidance. GST, the historical Income-tax Act 1961, complete current tax rates, rules and all amendments are not supplied. The 2025 Act collection does not establish commencement or applicability.
